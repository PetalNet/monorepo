## Question

What does reaching the end of this map look like? Does it cover only the voice head behind Home
Assistant, or also Janet's phone number, where clients and strangers call in?

## Resolution

Both, in one map. Parker chose the option putting the voice head and the phone number in a single effort.

The destination is a consolidated spec plus this decision map, covering one Janet reachable by voice
from every Home Assistant voice device he has and from a phone number, complete enough for main
Janet to build from without coming back to him.

This widens the effort past its Nov 11 framing, which treated the phone line as its own later
project, and it puts the "all of it goes through Home Assistant" ruling under strain, because Home
Assistant has no telephony. That tension is [How a call reaches Janet](how-a-call-reaches-janet.md).
