## Question

Nothing on the phone line can be tested until a number exists, so this is the task that unblocks the
whole second half of the map.

From the phone research (2026-10-19): a number plus self-hosted call control came to about six or
seven dollars a month, with a roughly twenty-one dollar one-time registration as a sole proprietor and
a one-to-two week vetting wait. Janet asked Parker to approve starting it on 2026-10-19 and the answer
never came, because the build was waiting on a usage reset.

Two reasons it must go to him and not be decided here: it costs money, and registration needs his
real identity information.

The vetting delay is the thing that matters for sequencing. If it is still one to two weeks, starting
it late makes it the critical path for everything phone-shaped, so it should be raised early even
though the phone slice is built second.
