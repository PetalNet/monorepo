## Platform

web

## Users

Friends opening Parker's link on phones and computers; Parker curates the resulting training data.

## Product Purpose

Collect varied real voices for Janet's wake-word recognition and speaker identification. A set contains the prototype's 25 wake phrases and 15 near-miss negatives, taking about three minutes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

First name and consent required; PetalNet sign-in optional. Preserve 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV and per-person clips.jsonl. Retain audio when reviewing. Save accepted takes locally and retry uploads. Janet alone applies deployment changes after review. No inference services.

## Brand Commitments

Follow the lab's Material 3, Svelte, DaisyUI, flat surfaces and accessible contrast conventions.

## Operating Context

Public HTTPS on heyjanet.petalcat.dev; NAS-backed storage on .14 through /services. Training runs on Parker's PC.

## Evidence on Hand

Prototype recorder.py supplies all prompts and Parker's 40 recordings. pos_17 is near-silent and must be flagged, preserved, and left undecided.

## Open Decisions
