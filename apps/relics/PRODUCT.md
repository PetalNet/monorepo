# Petal Relics

A Petal Relic is a party favor with its own friendly page. Tap its physical NFC tag or scan its QR code to open `/relics/{id}`. If it is unclaimed, type a friend's name and your first name. This phone becomes its owner without an account or PIN. The public page shows the name, first name, photo, number, type, color, birthday and one stable silly trait. Only the owning phone can rename it; renaming keeps the birthday. A quiet Pass it on action allows a new person to name it.

There is no collection game, missing-variety grid, browse page, public owner directory or guest completion count. Home is a short tag-tapping explainer. `/collection` redirects home. Existing device sessions and all physical tag URLs remain valid. Six legacy owners can still move ownership to a phone with their old name and PIN at the unlinked `/legacy` page. Existing custom nicknames are retained. Email recovery remains available at an unlinked address for earlier users.

The primary use is a phone immediately after tapping a physical tag. Parker chose B · Storybook: cream paper, Lora headings, Nunito body copy, Patrick Hand notes, olive actions and lighthearted language. Every guest state and staff task must work on small phones.

Staff sign in through Authentik with either `relics-admins` or `authentik Admins`. Their guided workshop adds a type, colors/photos, a tag quantity, then a writing checklist. Existing catalog editing, individual artwork, ownership reassignment, batch stories, sets and custom numbers remain. Chrome on Android can write and verify NFC tags; other browsers retain copying, QR and manual completion. Writing requires an explicit tap, exact readback and separate replacement consent; the foreground scanner stays active between writes to prevent ordinary URL dispatch.
