# Petal Relics

Physical collectible tags open a specimen page at /relics/{id}. Collectors claim by entering a name, keep a private collection on their device, optionally verify an email for restoration, and may release a relic for someone else. They have no account or PIN. Six legacy owners can migrate once using their existing name and PIN. Admins alone sign in through Authentik and manage the catalog, allocation ranges, tag writing and uploads. Existing IDs, artwork, URLs and ownership are preserved.

The primary scene is a phone immediately after tapping a physical tag. Art, specimen number, variety and a direct claim action lead. A personal grid shows owned cards and silhouettes for missing varieties. There is no public owner gallery or leaderboard.

Parker has selected a dark specimen card / museum label, artwork foremost, catalog numbering, and a light theme toggle. This direction and the product decisions are settled. The rewrite replaces the old interface entirely. Admin tools favor short, direct forms.
